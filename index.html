📏 Lab rules

  • Official sources only. Quotas and card requirements come from the platform's own pricing, FAQ or docs — never from comparison blogs.
  • “Not stated” is an answer. If a vendor does not publish a number, we write “not published” instead of inventing one.
  • Tested beats researched. A service only earns the Tested badge after a real signup and a real use, dated in the log.
  • Failures stay in the log. Permission errors, bot checks and disabled buttons are recorded, not hidden.
